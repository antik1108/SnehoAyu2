## 1. BRAND FOUNDATION

```
                     [ THE SNEHOAYU ZENITH ]
                           Empowerment

                      Care ◄────┼────► Science
                                │
                                ▼
                             Sustenance
```

### 1.1. Mission
To transition parents of preterm and NICU-graduated infants from fear to confidence by providing a supportive, medically precise, and culturally aligned mobile companion.

### 1.2. Vision
A world where newborn mortality and parental anxiety are diminished through the combination of professional medical science and human-centered design, beginning in India and expanding globally.

### 1.3. Brand Promise
"Every preterm baby deserves a gentle start; every mother deserves a calm guide." We promise absolute reliability, simple and clear language, and a calm space during high-stress moments.

### 1.4. Brand Values
1.  **Scientific Empathy (বিজ্ঞানসম্মত সহানুভূতি):** Every feature is backed by neonatal science but delivered with human warmth.
2.  **Radical Simplicity (সহজ সরলতা):** We simplify medical jargon to reduce the stress of tired parents.
3.  **Local Devotion (মাতৃভাষা নিষ্ঠা):** Designing first for Bengali speakers in India, treating localization as a fundamental respect, not an afterthought.
4.  **Quiet Dignity (শান্ত মর্যাদা):** We avoid flashing notices or alarmist designs. We respect the quiet environment of the nursery.

### 1.5. Brand Archetype
*   **Primary Archetype: The Caregiver (70%)**
    Nurturing, compassionate, protective, and generous. Focuses on support, longevity, and warmth.
*   **Secondary Archetype: The Sage (30%)**
    Wise, precise, educational, and research-backed. Focuses on clinical truth, numbers, charts, and facts.

### 1.6. Brand Voice & Tone

#### Brand Voice (Who We Are)
*   **Calm but Alert:** We speak with steady confidence. We never sound panicked or clinical.
*   **Clear and Direct:** We use plain language. We explain medical metrics clearly.
*   **Warm and Supportive:** We use words of gentle encouragement. We talk to mothers as equals in the care team.

#### Brand Tone (How We Speak in Different Contexts)

| Context | Brand Tone | Example copy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Emergency Vitals** | Direct, Actionable, Clear | "প্যাচটি সঠিক অবস্থানে বসান। ৩ সেকেন্ড চেপে রাখুন।" (Position the patch correctly. Hold for 3 seconds.) |
| **Daily Logs Success** | Encouraging, Warm | "আজকের ওজন নেওয়া সম্পন্ন হয়েছে। মায়া ভালো করছে!" (Today's weight logged. Maya is doing well!) |
| **Errors / Failed Inputs** | Reassuring, Calm, Non-judgmental | "এটি পুনরায় চেষ্টা করুন। আমরা আপনার তথ্য সযত্নে সংরক্ষণ করব।" (Try this again. We will save your data carefully.) |

## 2. BRAND STORY

```
               [ SNEHO (স্নেহ) ]  +  [ AYU (আয়ু) ]
             (Affection & Care)       (Life & Longevity)
                       \             /
                        \           /
                       [ SNEHOAYU ]
               (Scientific Care for New Life)
```

### 2.1. The Origin Story
SnehoAyu began in a neonatal intensive care unit (NICU) in West Bengal. A young mother sat by her preterm infant's incubator, overwhelmed by monitor alarms, complex medical terms, and handwritten logbooks. The discharge instructions were confusing, leaving her with the daunting task of tracking breathing rates, feeds, and temperatures at home on her own.

We realized that while medical technology had advanced inside the hospital, the digital support for parents returning home remained cold, complex, or unavailable. SnehoAyu was created to fill this gap: a digital bridge that translates clinical data into quiet, supportive guidance.

### 2.2. The Meaning of SnehoAyu
*   **Sneho (স্নেহ):** Affection, care, and nurturing love. It represents a mother’s touch and a nurse's care.
*   **Ayu (আয়ু):** Life, health, and longevity. It represents our scientific goal: supporting healthy development for a long, healthy life.
*   **SnehoAyu:** Nurturing love combined with neonatal science to guide young lives forward.

### 2.3. How We Help

#### How It Helps Mothers
*   Replaces guesswork with clear tracking (weight, feeding intervals, temperature).
*   Translates vital numbers into clear, readable statuses (like "Normal" or "Needs Attention").
*   Provides immediate, clear guidance when vitals fall out of range, reducing panic.

#### How It Helps Healthcare Workers & Researchers
*   Generates clean, standardized charts that simplify checkups.
*   Provides researchers with high-quality, anonymous biometric data to improve preterm care practices across India.

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY

### 3.1. Visual Personality
The visual system is clean and comforting. We avoid busy graphics, choosing instead clear layouts that focus attention on what matters most.

### 3.2. Shapes & Geometry
*   **Organic Curves:** Enforce a standard corner radius of 20px to 24px on cards, buttons, and sheets. Avoid sharp corners, which can feel harsh and clinical.
*   **Encircling Shapes:** Use soft circles to surround vitals and progress rings. This creates a visual sense of protection and care.

```
       Rounded Geometry                    Harsh/Clinical
     ┌──────────────────┐               ┌──────────────────┐
     │  24px rounded     │               │  Sharp 0px corner│
     │  feels soft &    │      vs       │  feels cold &    │
     │  approachable    │               │  bureaucratic    │
     └──────────────────┘               └──────────────────┘
```

### 3.3. Illustration & Iconography Style
*   **Minimalist Line Drawings:** Use simple, clean line drawings with single-color brand accents.
*   **No Playful Cartoon Characters:** The app is a medical tool, not a game. Icons must be simple, professional, and clear.
*   **Flat Icon Sets:** Use thin, clean icon lines (like Lucide React) with a stroke width of 1.5px or 2px.

### 3.4. Pattern & Texture Rules
*   **Clean Backgrounds:** Backgrounds must remain solid (`bg-background`). Do not use gradients, textured layers, or photographic background fills.
*   **High Contrast:** Rely on clear borders (`border-border`) and generous whitespace to divide information.

### 4.1. The Primary Logo
The primary logo combines our custom symbol (an infant wrapped in a protecting heart) with the Bengali logotype **"স্নেহ আয়ু"**.
*   **Font:** Hind Siliguri Bold.
*   **Horizontal Layout:** Used for headers, desktop views, and documents.
*   **Stacked Layout:** Used for splash screens, app icons, and onboarding cards.

### 4.2. Icon-Only Logo, Favicon, & App Icon
*   **Icon-Only Symbol:** A heart shield enclosing a stylized baby wrap.
*   **Favicon:** Clean teal background circle with the white inner baby wrap path.
*   **App Icon:** A soft teal background with a centered, high-contrast white symbol, utilizing no shadows or glossy highlights.

### 4.3. Safe Area & Minimum Size
*   **Safe Area:** Enforce a clear margin around the logo equal to 50% of the logo's width. No text, images, or page borders may enter this zone.
*   **Minimum Size:**
    *   *Digital:* 32px height on mobile views.
    *   *Print:* 20mm height.

### 4.4. Improper Usage
*   Never use colors outside our brand palette (e.g., do not render the logo in blue, orange, or black).
*   Never apply drop shadows or glass effects directly to the logo.
*   Never stretch, rotate, or crop the logo path.

## 5. COLOR & GEOMETRY PSYCHOLOGY

```
          [ TEAL ]                      [ ROSE ]
     Clinical Precision,           Maternal Affection,
     Calm Security, Medical        Warmth, Nurturing Love,
     Trustworthiness               Kangaroo Mother Care
```

*   **Why Teal?** Teal combines the calming qualities of blue with the renewing energy of green. It establishes clinical trust and focus, helping parents feel secure in the app's guidance.
*   **Why Rose?** Rose represents warmth, skin-to-skin connection (Kangaroo Mother Care), and breastmilk feeding. It adds human warmth, balancing the clinical feel of the teal.
*   **Why Whitespace?** Sleep-deprived parents can easily become overwhelmed by cluttered interfaces. Generous whitespace gives the eyes room to rest, helping users focus on one vital measurement at a time.
*   **Why Rounded Shapes?** Sharp corners signal danger or caution to the brain. Soft, rounded geometry (20px to 24px) feels welcoming and safe.
*   **Why Minimalism?** A minimalist design is easier to read in dim light (like a baby’s nursery at night) and reduces cognitive load during stressful moments.

## 6. IMAGERY SYSTEM

Our imagery focuses on clean, respectful, and authentic representation.

### 6.1. Photography Guidelines
*   **Real Environments:** Show mothers and nurses in natural, warm settings. Avoid polished stock photos that feel artificial.
*   **Soft Natural Light:** Use warm, gentle lighting. Avoid harsh studio flashes.
*   **Respectful Representation:** Photos must be culturally authentic, showing families in realistic Indian home and clinical settings with quiet dignity.

```
      Allowed: Real & Warm                  Forbidden: Artificial
 ┌───────────────────────────┐         ┌───────────────────────────┐
 │ Real mother holding child │         │ Staged studio stock photo │
 │ in natural lighting.      │    vs   │ with heavy makeup and     │
 │ Focus is on connection.   │         │ perfect artificial light. │
 └───────────────────────────┘         └───────────────────────────┘
```

### 6.2. Visual Assets Matrix

| Asset Type | Allowed | Forbidden |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Photos** | Natural expressions, warm lighting, real-world Indian mother-baby scenes. | Staged stock photos, white-coated models, hyper-saturated colors. |
| **Illustrations** | Simple, clear line drawings that explain care steps. | Cartoons, child-like doodles, bright decorative graphics. |
| **3D Graphics** | Clean, scientific representations of medical procedures or vitals. | Decorative 3D icons, heavy shadows, glossy textures. |
| **Icons** | Thin, clean lines with a 1.5px stroke width. | Multi-color icons, heavy fills, tech-style icons. |

## 7. COPYWRITING GUIDELINES

Every sentence must be clear, warm, and easy to understand.

### 7.1. Tone & Style Rules
*   **Choose Bengali First:** Write in natural, clear Bengali. Avoid direct translations from English that sound robotic.
*   **Use Active Verbs:** Tell the user exactly what to do. Focus on clear, encouraging steps.
*   **Break Down Jargon:** Explain medical terms simply. For example, instead of "Enter neonatal spO2", use "আপনার শিশুর অক্সিজেনের মাত্রা লিখুন" (Enter your baby's oxygen level).

### 7.2. Copy Templates

#### Vitals Logs & Success Checks
*   *Bengali:* "ওজন নেওয়া হয়েছে। মায়া গত সপ্তাহ থেকে ৫০ গ্রাম ওজন বৃদ্ধি করেছে।"
*   *English:* "Weight logged. Maya has gained 50g since last week."

#### Critical Warning Messaging
*   *Bengali:* "অক্সিজেন লেভেল কম (৮৮%)। আপনার শিশুর শ্বাস-প্রশ্বাস পরীক্ষা করুন। যদি ঠোঁট নীল দেখায়, তবে বিশেষজ্ঞকে কল করুন।"
*   *English:* "Oxygen is low (88%). Check your baby's breathing. If lips look blue, call your specialist immediately."

#### Research Feedbacks
*   *Bengali:* "আপনার দেওয়া তথ্য ডাক্তারদের সাহায্য করছে। ধন্যবাদ।"
*   *English:* "Your inputs are helping doctors improve care. Thank you."

## 8. EMOTIONAL STATES

We map the UI density and motion flow to the user's emotional state during different activities.

```
       [ Onboarding ] ─────────► [ Vitals Logging ] ─────────► [ Emergency Vitals ]
        Parent Anxiety:          Parent Anxiety:               Parent Anxiety:
             HIGH                     MEDIUM                        EXTREME
        Visual Density:          Visual Density:               Visual Density:
             LOW                      LOW                           VERY LOW
        Color Theme:             Color Theme:                  Color Theme:
             Teal (Calm)              Teal / Rose (Warm)            Amber / Red (Action)
```

### 8.1. Onboarding (State: Anxious Hope)
*   **UI Feeling:** Welcoming, clean, and simple.
*   **Visual Elements:** Slow entry transitions, large input fields, and single primary actions.
*   **Copy:** Welcoming and reassuring.

### 8.2. Vitals Logging (State: Focused Routine)
*   **UI Feeling:** Fast, clear, and easy to use.
*   **Visual Elements:** Standard card layouts, clear confirmation buttons, and simple success states.
*   **Copy:** Clear and encouraging.

### 8.3. Emergency Vitals (State: Extreme Alert)
*   **UI Feeling:** High contrast, clear, and direct.
*   **Visual Elements:** Solid background highlights, zero decorative graphics, and a single direct button to call for help.
*   **Copy:** Direct, step-by-step instructions.

## 11. BRAND GOVERNANCE

To scale the brand cleanly across developers and designers, we establish clear rules.

### 11.1. Core Elements (Non-Negotiable)
These elements must not be changed without brand director approval:
*   The primary color definitions (`Teal #0f766e` and `Rose #f43f5e`).
*   The primary font (`Hind Siliguri`) for Bengali UI text.
*   The touch target minimum size of 48px.

### 11.2. Brand Review Checklist
Use this checklist when building or reviewing new screens:
*   [ ] Does the layout follow the 60-30-10 whitespace rule?
*   [ ] Are all touch targets at least 48px square?
*   [ ] Are all Bengali texts using the `font-bengali` class?
*   [ ] Do biometric displays include clear text evaluations (e.g. "Normal") alongside the numbers?
*   [ ] Have you avoided the use of glassmorphism and transparent overlays?
*   [ ] Are error messages reassuring and clear, avoiding alarmist language?
